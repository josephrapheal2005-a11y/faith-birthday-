A Poor Work of Art

For Ukwela Faith Ojochebo

what do I write for you?
what do I write about you?
i doubt I'd be able to do the many numbers like you did.
just like rays and light,
i start scattering and fading with the distance.
so I'd try to keep this short, yet packed full!

i don't know how we got close,
but you're a destiny friend.

that day when I chatted you asking how we'd go about the reading stuff for Pensword in 2026...
i was slightly thrown off by your profile picture.
yeah, you really need to change that stuff.
but then I loved how confident the voice behind the very child-like face sounded.

and then when I suggested reading a month and writing the next month without reading and you said, "how wouldn't we read for a month?" then laughed,
i was like,
who does she think she is!

but then came so close to obsessed with finding out who you really are.

i told ma overflowing ink how amazing you are,
and sir Sammy was surprised you posted me on my birthday...
well, I was more surprised than he was.
you even posted me a few hours before the actual birthday.

i've never been celebrated the way you did that day,
and I saved your birthday wish on my phone,
the only birthday wish I saved.

i love how eager you are to help.
i love how early you're taking your life serious.
you're the kid sister I never had,
and I want to see you excel like mad.

i don't love how you call me obsessed fan.
we started off as mutual fans, alright, but...
how long before you realize that fans don't tell each other about JAMB scores and IJMB?

stop making me beg this year.
stop crying too, crying too much should be banned, walahi!

you've got a pretty, pretty face.
how the hell do you wake up and decide your face is still resting?!

it's hard to compete against books with you.

i hope that by now, we both agree that you've read more books in life than I.
you gotta achieve the 500 nonfiction goals.

now I want you to make a wish,
to God, then me.
tell me that one thing you want from me.
you've gently pushed me into so many things,
it'd just be a little way of paying you back.

happiest of birthdays, sis...

i see you reaching and touching summits I gave up trying to reach.

make sure you enjoy your day.

birthday joke: A yes to Winfred would be a perfect birthday gift to him 😭

enjoy your day like mad.

despite being a poet,
this is quite a poor work of art.

Written especially for you,Songspirer
